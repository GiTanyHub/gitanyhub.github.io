Privacy policy — rclone YouTube

Last updated: 17 November 2026

rclone YouTube is used only by its owner, on the owner's own computer, with the owner's own Google account.

  • The app accesses Google Drive only to copy, list and check the owner's own backup files.
  • No data is sent to any server other than Google Drive. Nothing is sold, shared or used for advertising.
  • The access token is stored only locally on the owner's computer, in the rclone configuration file.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
